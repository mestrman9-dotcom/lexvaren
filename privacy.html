Privacy Policy

LEX VAREN: Covert Ops — last updated 29 October 2026

In short

The game does not collect or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it plays offline, apart from the in-app purchase, which Apple handles.

What stays on your device

The game stores on your device only: language, control settings, music mute, progress checkpoints and whether Part 1 has been purchased. None of it leaves the device, and it is removed when you delete the game.

In-app purchase

Completing Part 1 is an in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple (App Store). We receive no payment details or account information; the purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Restoring a purchase is also handled by Apple.

What we do not do

We do not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we share nothing with third parties.

Children

We collect no data from anyone, including children.
