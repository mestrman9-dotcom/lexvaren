اشتريت الجزء الأول على جهاز آخر أو أعدت التثبيت؟ اضغط «استعادة المشتريات» في شاشة البداية بنفس حساب Apple.

هل يُحفظ تقدّمي؟ نعم، عند نقاط الحفظ، ويظهر زر «متابعة» في شاشة البداية.

FAQ

How do I change the language or controls? Open the menu button at the top of the screen, then Settings. A left-handed layout is available.

How do I mute the music? The music-note button at the top mutes music only and keeps the sound effects.

Spotted and can't get away? Break line of sight and hide; if you stay exposed for 35 seconds the response unit arrives and RETRY appears.

What is free and what is paid? The download and the Building 09 mission are free. Completing Part 1 is one permanent in-app purchase.

Bought Part 1 on another device or reinstalled? Tap RESTORE PURCHASE on the start screen, with the same Apple ID.

Is my progress saved? Yes, at checkpoints; CONTINUE appears on the start screen.

Does the game need the internet? No, it plays offline, except to buy or restore the purchase.
